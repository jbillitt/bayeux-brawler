Drop your recorded TROJAN-BURST sounds here (.wav, .ogg or .mp3 — any filenames).
Every audio file in this folder is loaded at startup and one is picked at random
each time the belly of the Great Horse splits open and the spearmen come out:
splintering timber, a war cry from inside. This layers OVER the synthesized
crunch rather than replacing it. If this folder has no audio files, nothing extra
plays and nothing breaks.
