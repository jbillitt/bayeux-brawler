# Bayeux Brawler — Game Overview & Agent Reference

> A comedic medieval roguelike combat simulator rendered in the style of the Bayeux Tapestry.

---

## Game Concept

**Bayeux Brawler** is a single-player Android game where you build a custom Norman knight from an absurd catalog of medieval weapon parts, armor, and companions, then watch him fight waves of Saxon enemies in auto-battler combat rendered to look like an animated Bayeux Tapestry embroidery.

The core loop:
1. **Customize** — Pick a weapon head + handle, shield, armor, helmet, hair, size, name
2. **Fight** — Watch your knight auto-battle escalating Saxon waves on a scrolling battlefield
3. **Level Up** — Choose roguelike upgrades: extra blades, armor layers, followers, mounts
4. **Die or Conquer** — Push for high scores with risk-reward gear choices (naked = 10x multiplier)

**Development Rule:** Always increment the bugs fix version number on every change (e.g. v0.2.6 Wessex, v0.2.7 Wessex).

## Genre & Influences

- **Auto-battler roguelike** — You build, RNG fights
- **Equipment crafting** — Mix-and-match modular weapons (16 heads × 9 handles = 144 combos)
- **Risk-reward scoring** — Less armor = more points
- **Visual style** — Bayeux Tapestry embroidery aesthetic (stitched outlines, linen textures, medieval color palette)
- **Audio** — Fully procedural medieval music that layers up as you progress
- **Comedy** — Dog Latin combat barks, absurd weapon combos (spiked flail on a cart wheel), TTS vocalization

## Player Agent

The player's knight is a `FighterState` entity with:
- **Position**: moves along a 1D battlefield (0–1000)
- **Combat**: auto-attacks when in weapon range, cooldown-based
- **Stats derived from gear**: heavier gear = slower but tankier, lighter = glass cannon
- **Size**: affects hitbox, damage (squared scaling), speed, health
- **Dual wielding**: available when no shield equipped, doubles attack speed
- **Mounting**: rides a warhorse when Destrier unlocked (suppresses legs, raises body, speed boost)

### Stat System

| Stat | Formula | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Damage | `(slash + pierce + blunt) × size² × level_scale` | Higher = kills faster |
| Attack Speed | `base_delay × weight_factor × shield_factor × handle_penalty` | Lower = faster swings |
| Move Speed | `(base / size) × (1 - mass_penalty) × (1 + speedBoost)` | Pixels/sec movement |
| Armor | `(armor_def + helm_def) × size + level_bonus` | Flat damage reduction |
| Score Mult | `1.0 + naked_bonus + no_helm + no_shield + light_weapon` | Up to 10x for full naked |

## Enemy Agents (Saxons)

Generated by `generateRandomSaxon()` with level-scaling:

| Level | Enemy Characteristics |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Basic gear, small size variation, slow |
| 3–4 | Better weapons, occasional shields, medium size |
| 5–6 | Mixed ranged/melee, 15–35% mounted, named champions |
| 7–8 | Heavy armor, dual wielders, 35–60% mounted |
| 8+ | Full variety, 60% mounted, scale armor, great helms |

## Level-Up System

After winning a battle, the player chooses from randomized upgrades:

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| `attachment` | Extra blade/spike welded to weapon (+50% dmg from that head) |
| `extension` | Handle extension (+0.35 reach per extension) |
| `armor` | Extra armor layer (stacks defense) |
| `follower` | Unlock an ancillary NPC |
| `mount` | Unlock the Destrier warhorse |

## Music Progression

The procedural composer builds the soundtrack across levels:

| Level | Mood | Instruments |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Melancholic, sparse | Solo harp arpeggios |
| 2 | Warming | + Lute chords |
| 3 | Grounding | + Drone strings |
| 4 | Martial pulse | + Bodhrán war drum |
| 5 | Melodic complexity | + Recorder counter-melody |
| 6 | Rhythmic drive | + Tambourine |
| 7 | Grandeur | + Horn organum |
| 8+ | Full orchestra | + Psaltery (ORCHESTRAL) or fanfare accents |

Two seeded "destiny" paths shape the entire run's musical character:
- **FANFARE**: Rising melodic bias, strong duple meter, bright brass
- **ORCHESTRAL**: Descending arch, triple lilt, Greensleeves-esque sweep
