Drop your recorded BOMB-EXPLOSION sounds here (.wav, .ogg or .mp3 - any filenames).
Every audio file in this folder is loaded at startup and one is picked at random
each time a Far-Eastern bomb bursts: on a man, on the ground, or against a siege
gate. Short, dry cracks work better than long booms - several can go off at once.
If this folder has no audio files, nothing plays and nothing breaks.
