Drop your recorded GOOSE sounds here (.wav, .ogg or .mp3 - any filenames).
Every audio file in this folder is loaded at startup and one is picked at random
each time a weapon with a goose lashed to it lands a hit - the goose getting hold
of somebody. On-hit only: it never honks idly. Angry hisses and wing-beats suit it
better than a farmyard honk. If this folder has no audio files, nothing plays and
nothing breaks.
